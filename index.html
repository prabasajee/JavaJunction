Est. 1941

Who We Are

Our family at JavaJunction has been crafting exceptional coffee experiences for over eight decades.

Founded by our grandfather, Carlos Ramírez, in 1941, JavaJunction began as a small neighborhood coffee shop with a big dream: to serve the perfect cup of coffee. Carlos's innovative approach to coffee roasting and his commitment to quality quickly made JavaJunction a beloved destination.

Today, we continue his legacy with the same passion and dedication, using his time-tested methods while embracing modern techniques to bring you coffee that's both traditional and contemporary.
