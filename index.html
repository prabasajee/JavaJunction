Brewing Methods

Master the art of coffee making

French Press

1. Heat water to 200°F 2. Coarse grind coffee 3. Steep for 4 minutes 4. Press slowly
⏱️ 4 minutes

Pour Over

1. Medium-fine grind 2. Wet filter paper 3. Pour in circles 4. Control flow rate
⏱️ 3-4 minutes

Espresso Machine

1. Fine grind coffee 2. Tamp evenly 3. Extract 25-30 sec 4. Watch the crema
⏱️ 30 seconds

Cold Brew

1. Coarse grind coffee 2. Cold water steep 3. Wait 12-24 hours 4. Filter and serve
⏱️ 12-24 hours
